# Ashok Hero: customer acquisition plan

Prepared 12 September 2026. These are proposed actions and experiment budgets, not
launched campaigns or forecasts. No advertising money has been spent by this task.

## What is operational today

- Public website: https://ityashag.github.io/ashokhero/
- Model enquiries open a prefilled message to the showroom WhatsApp number.
- Call and directions links are present.
- Campaign information and interaction events exist in the frontend.
- Repository Actions secrets were empty on inspection: no lead webhook is configured
  there. No central CRM, analytics account or outbound messaging automation was
  connected by this work. Clicking WhatsApp does not prove a message was sent.
- The website cannot identify the names or phone numbers of anonymous visitors.
  People become contactable leads when they share details or contact the showroom.

## Customer promise

Position Ashok Hero as a helpful local showroom: "Your Hero, your budget — clear
quotes, test rides and local service in Nakatia, Bareilly."

Make the offer concrete: confirmed on-road quote, available colour, exchange
inspection or a booked test ride. Explain price components clearly. Publish only
showroom-approved prices and offers; finance approval and availability need checking.
Use consistent business name, logo, address and phone across the website, Google,
WhatsApp, showroom signage and social accounts. Use real showroom/team photos;
get permission before publishing identifiable customer delivery photos.

## First week: close the enquiry loop

1. Assign one staff member to incoming website/WhatsApp leads during business hours.
   Aim to respond within five minutes when staffed; clearly display after-hours expectations.
2. Connect a private CRM or protected form backend before running ads. Record name,
   phone, model, need, source/campaign, consent, assigned staff, next follow-up time,
   appointment, outcome and sale value. Restrict access to staff who need it.
3. Use stages: New → Contacted → Qualified → Test ride booked → Visited → Sold/Lost.
   Record the lost reason; deduplicate returning customers by phone in the private CRM.
4. Add a visible privacy notice and separate optional marketing opt-in. Permission
   to answer an enquiry is not permission for indefinite promotional messages.
5. Connect GA4/GTM and conversion tracking with appropriate consent. Never put names,
   phones or email addresses into ordinary analytics events or page URLs.
6. Distinguish WhatsApp/call clicks from confirmed conversations, valid saved leads,
   attended test rides and actual sales. Staff should record offline outcomes.

## Google Business Profile: free discovery and trust

- Confirm categories, showroom hours, holiday hours, address, phone and website.
- Tag the website link:
  https://ityashag.github.io/ashokhero/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=business_profile
- Each week publish a useful model walkthrough, service tip, and a real showroom or
  delivery update (with customer permission). Hindi-first content suits the proposed local campaign.
- Ask all actual delivery/service customers for an honest review; reply helpfully.
  Do not buy reviews, offer rewards or request reviews only from happy customers.
- Measure profile calls, website visits and directions requests alongside actual
  showroom enquiries. Better rankings or a fixed lead volume cannot be guaranteed.

Google explains local ranking as relevance, distance and prominence:
https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en
Review guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en

## Weeks 2–3: controlled paid experiment

Proposed example cap, only after budget approval: ₹10,000 media spend over 14 days,
excluding taxes/provider fees/content production. Start with roughly 70% Google
Search and 30% Meta click-to-WhatsApp. A smaller budget should start with one channel.

- Google: local purchase-intent searches such as "Hero showroom Bareilly",
  "Splendor on road price Bareilly" and "Xtreme 125R test ride Bareilly".
- Target people in the dealership's actual service area; begin with Bareilly and
  nearby reachable areas, refined using the locations of customers who visit.
- Separate the main models into ad groups, match each ad to its enquiry offer,
  review search terms, and exclude irrelevant jobs/manual/download searches.
- Meta: two or three short real-bike videos with one clear CTA, such as "Ask for
  today's on-road quote" or "Book a showroom test ride". Use a dedicated campaign
  message to help staff identify the source.
- Schedule staffed response coverage. Check spend and lead quality daily. Stop or
  revise ads producing irrelevant enquiries or exceeding the approved loss cap.
- Do not automate broad WhatsApp broadcasts. For Business Platform automation,
  obtain opt-in, provide opt-out and use approved templates when required.

## Follow-up that helps the customer decide

First reply: confirm model, availability and requested quote; ask for a preferred
test-ride time. Offer a clear next step rather than repeated generic promotions.

For an opted-in enquiry, make a relevant follow-up the next working day if needed;
send a booked-appointment reminder at the agreed time. Respect STOP immediately.
Use the official Business Platform for unattended outbound automation; its
template/window requirements also apply to follow-ups. Keep human help available.

## Weekly sales scorecard

Track spend, unique valid enquiries, qualified enquiries, appointments, visits,
sales, response time, source and gross contribution after variable selling costs.

- Cost per qualified lead = ad spend / qualified leads.
- Lead-to-sale rate = sales / qualified leads.
- Ad acquisition cost = ad spend / acquired customers.
- Fully loaded acquisition cost includes content, tools and allocated follow-up costs.
- An affordable acquisition cost must leave the required profit from each sale.

Illustration only: if contribution per sale is ₹5,000 and 5% of qualified leads buy,
the break-even ad cost per qualified lead is ₹250 before other acquisition costs.
Set a lower target to leave profit. Use actual dealership margins and observed
conversion rates to decide whether to increase spending; clicks alone are insufficient.

## Decisions needed before launch

Confirm the ad budget, WhatsApp Business number/account, responsible salesperson,
actual service area, stock/approved offers, and account access for Google/Meta/CRM.
Connect and test lead capture first; obtain approval before spending or publishing ads.
